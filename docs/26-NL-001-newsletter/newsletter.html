Closedown season

The other half of the business goes into its busiest run of the year.

400+

pools to close down before the cold sets in

Four hundred and counting, and the weather sets the deadline, not us. Every one shut down properly is a pool that opens clean in spring and a customer who rings us rather than somebody else. It is the biggest single run of work in the calendar — get into a rhythm early and it looks after itself.
